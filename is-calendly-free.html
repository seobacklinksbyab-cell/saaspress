Is Calendly Free? What You Get on the Free Plan (and What You Don't)

By AdminNovember 9, 2026Reading Time: 5 minutes

Yes, Calendly is free. It has a free plan that costs $0 and does not expire. The catch is that the free plan is built for one simple use: one type of meeting, booked on one calendar. For many freelancers that is enough. For others it stops working the day they want to offer a second type of call.

This guide explains exactly what you get on the free plan, what you do not, what the paid plans cost, and how to decide in two minutes whether you need to pay. Prices below are per seat per month and were checked against recent pricing roundups in October 2026, so confirm the final number on Calendly's own pricing page before you buy.

Short answer: is Calendly free?

Calendly offers a free plan with no end date and no credit card needed to start. You can share a booking link and take as many bookings as you like. The limit is not the number of meetings. The limit is the number of event types you can create, and on the free plan that number is one.

What the Calendly free plan includes

  • One event type: for example a single 30-minute meeting that clients can book.
  • Unlimited meetings: there is no cap on how many people can book that one event type.
  • One connected calendar: Calendly checks it so people cannot book you when you are busy.
  • Your own availability: you choose the days and hours when people can book.
  • Video conferencing links: you can attach a video call link to your meetings.

The limit that matters most: one event type

An event type is a kind of meeting with its own length and settings. Think of a 15-minute intro call, a 30-minute catch-up and a 60-minute working session as three different event types.

On the free plan you only get one of these. That works well if every client meets you in the same way. It becomes a problem when you want a short free intro call for new leads and a longer paid session for existing clients, because those need two separate links with two different lengths.

A common workaround is to pick one length that suits everyone, such as 30 minutes, and use it for all meetings. If that feels too limiting, you are the person the paid plan is for.

What the free plan does not include

  • More than one event type: unlimited event types start on the Standard plan.
  • Group booking: this is listed with Standard, not with Free.
  • Team scheduling: sharing meetings across several people, including round-robin, starts on the Teams plan.

How much do the paid Calendly plans cost?

  • Standard: $10 per seat per month when billed yearly, or $12 per seat per month when billed monthly.
  • Teams: $16 per seat per month when billed yearly, or $20 per seat per month when billed monthly.
  • Enterprise: custom pricing for large companies, with figures starting at roughly $15,000 a year in recent roundups.

Calendly also sells add-ons on top of Standard and Teams, which raise the monthly price per seat. If you only want to schedule meetings, you can ignore them.

Is the Calendly free plan enough for freelancers?

For many freelancers, yes. The free plan is enough when:

  • You only need one kind of meeting, for example a standard 30-minute call.
  • You work alone and do not need to share booking with a team.
  • You use one calendar for your work schedule.
  • You do not need to run group sessions.

It is worth paying for Standard when you need different meeting lengths for different clients, or when you start running group calls. At $10 a month billed yearly, that is $120 a year, so make sure the extra event types will save you real back-and-forth emails before you upgrade.

How to decide in two minutes

  1. Write down every kind of meeting you take with clients, such as intro call, project kickoff and review.
  2. Count how many different lengths or settings they need.
  3. If the answer is one, stay on the free plan. If it is two or more, price out Standard.
  4. Check Calendly's pricing page for the current monthly and yearly price before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Is Calendly really free forever?

The free plan has no time limit, so you can keep using it as long as the one event type is enough for you. Calendly can change its plans in future, so check the pricing page if you are planning around it.

Do the people who book me need to pay?

No. The person booking a meeting does not need a Calendly account or a paid plan. Only the person who sets up the booking link needs one.

Can I use Calendly free with more than one calendar?

The free plan connects one calendar. If you manage separate work and personal calendars and need both checked, review the paid plans.
