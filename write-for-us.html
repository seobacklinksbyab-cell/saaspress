What we publish

We accept articles about AI tools for freelancers, SaaS comparisons, and free software with practical tutorials. Every article must be useful to a reader who wants to solve a real problem.

We accept

  • Original articles of 1,000 words or more
  • Clear headings, short paragraphs, real examples
  • Topics that fit our three topics above
  • 1 or 2 relevant links to trusted sources
  • A short author bio (up to 50 words)

We do not accept

  • Copied, spun, or already published content
  • Unedited AI text with no real insight
  • Casino, adult, loans, or other restricted topics
  • Articles that exist only to place a link
  • More than 2 outbound links

Link policy: paid guest posts are labeled as sponsored, and links in them use the rel="sponsored" attribute, as Google requires. We may edit articles for clarity and reject any submission that does not meet these guidelines.
